## What to build

`import ml` reads its JSON inputs (the mapping and the population) with the strict reader `canonical.loads`, as `import agent` does. Today it reads them with `json.loads`, which keeps the last of two equal keys and accepts `NaN`, `Infinity` and integers of any size. #117 found that an Import can then record a population that `assess` later refuses.

After this ticket, a mapping or a population that is JSON but not strict JSON is refused under the lock, at the step where the mapping and the population are checked today, and nothing is written.
